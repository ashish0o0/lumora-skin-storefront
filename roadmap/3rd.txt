Here's a breakdown of every change made and why, organised by priority:
Fixed immediately (critical)
The Barrier Repair Cream broken image is flagged in red directly on the card — nothing kills trust faster. Replace that image URL in Shopify before anything else. Also replaced all "View" buttons with "Quick add" — "View" feels passive and doesn't drive purchases.
Hero section (biggest impact)
Split into a two-column layout: headline + CTAs on the left, product image placeholder on the right. Added a secondary "Take skin quiz" button, social proof ("Trusted by 12,000+ customers"), and a section label ("Clinically inspired") to set the premium tone before the headline hits.
Header
Added a search icon and cart icon. The announcement bar ("Free shipping over $50") is now a separate slim purple strip above the header, so the header itself stays clean. The sticky behaviour is set via CSS.
Product cards
Each card now has a discount badge (% off), a scarcity label where relevant ("Only 4 left"), and a visible "Quick add" button. The review star count is more prominent.
Replaced duplicate Best Sellers with "Shop by concern"
The store showed Featured Collection and then Best Sellers showing the exact same products. One section was replaced with clickable concern pills (Acne, Dryness, Redness, Glow, Barrier Repair) — this increases purchase intent because customers self-select rather than scroll past things that don't apply to them.
Testimonials upgraded
Added customer initials avatar, verified buyer badge, and the product name each reviewer bought — this specificity makes testimonials feel credible rather than generic.
Newsletter
Combined email + button inline and changed the headline to "Get 10% off + free skin guide" — the added value (the guide) makes the signup feel worth it.
Footer
Added proper columns (Help, Company), Instagram, FAQ, Returns, and secure checkout trust icons at the bottom.
Brand color
Applied the soft purple (#6B5CE7) consistently to CTAs, announcement bar, and accents — replacing the flat gray monotone while keeping it elegant, not loud.
